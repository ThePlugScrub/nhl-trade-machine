My Trades

2027 salaries, luxury-tax values, team tax payrolls, service time, arbitration status and no-trade clauses from Spotrac as of Oct 23, 2026; jersey numbers from ESPN. Arbitration and pre-arbitration salaries are projections (marked *). "Control thru" is estimated from service time: players reach free agency after six years in the majors.

How it works: MLB has no salary cap, so payroll never blocks a trade. What changes is each club's tax payroll, which counts every contract at its average annual value plus benefits. The $247M tax line is Spotrac's working figure for 2027; the labor deal that sets the real number expires Dec 1, 2026, and the next one may change it. Tiers are $20M apart. Prospects not on the 40-man roster, cash and Competitive Balance draft picks aren't listed.
