My Trades

Cap numbers, base salaries, signing-bonus proration, team cap space and 2027 draft picks (with projected spots from current standings) from OverTheCap as of Oct 23, 2026; positions, ages and jersey numbers from ESPN. Practice-squad players can't be traded and aren't listed.

How in-season trades count: the new team takes on the rest of the player's 2026 base salary (the weekly paychecks still to come). The old team keeps the signing-bonus charge it already counted this year, and any bonus money spread over future years becomes dead money on its 2027 cap (the post-June 1 rule). A trade is blocked if either team ends up over the cap. Not modeled: per-game roster bonuses, incentives, offset language and future-year cap effects.
